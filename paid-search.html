Paid search / Google Ads planning

Planning Google Search ads on a limited budget

I used Semrush to research how people search for motorcycles and built an Excel sheet comparing search volume and estimated cost per click. I grouped searches by buyer intent and wrote responsive search ads for distinct audiences. The client also chose a campaign targeting broader terms with higher search volume and higher estimated costs.

From keyword research to campaign measurement

Research the funnel

I mapped broad terms like “motorcycles” through to specific searches such as “used motorcycles under €1,000.”

Match ads to buyer intent

I identified reachable audiences and wrote headlines and descriptions around their priorities, including condition, motorcycle type and budget.

Review campaign performance

The measurement stage compares spend, clicks and recorded conversions by ad group to understand which searches lead to buyer action.

Campaign planning evidence

My contribution

Choosing keywords within budget

I compared estimated click costs with the specificity of each search. A buyer who names a price ceiling gives us a clearer message to test. I used the spreadsheet to make that trade-off visible alongside the client’s higher-volume targets.

Connecting copy to landing pages

I wrote variations for each group and checked whether the destination delivered what the headline promised. For a budget-specific search, I looked at how easily the buyer could find motorcycles within that price range.
