B2B SEO strategy / Content prioritization

How I addressed a drop in organic clicks and prioritized sales pages

Organic clicks were falling at a supply chain consulting firm, while its informational articles continued to attract readers. I linked those articles to relevant sales pages and drafted new titles and meta descriptions to help more buyers find those pages in Google and click through.

Proposed editorial roadmap

I reviewed 58 plan items and identified 17 priorities for the team.

Now · Improve sales pages

  • Refresh the search titles and descriptions on pages that lost visibility.
  • Give readers of popular articles a direct link to the service that answers their question.

Next · Answer buyers’ questions

  • Use questions buyers ask when choosing a firm to guide sales-page updates.
  • Explain who each service is for and what buyers can expect when they hire the team.

Later · Find content and link opportunities

  • Compare competitors’ coverage with the firm’s content. Prioritize gaps the consultants can fill with first-hand examples.
  • Find industry publications and associations that link to competitors and pitch them a relevant case study or expert article.

How I set priorities

I found where clicks were falling

  • US organic clicks fell from 123 to 82 in 28 days, while impressions rose 35%.
  • I compared pages and prioritized sales pages that had lost visibility.

I identified what buyers wanted to know

  • I grouped 941 searches of eight or more words by what buyers were trying to decide.
  • One comparison article accounted for 71% of those impressions. I linked it to relevant sales pages.

I chose which rankings to improve first

  • Positions 1–3: protect strong rankings. Positions 4–10: look for opportunities to reach the top three.
  • Positions 11–20: focus updates on pages closest to page one.

I turned findings into page updates

  • I drafted new titles and meta descriptions for 54 service and industry pages.
  • I recommended adding case studies so buyers could see how the firm had helped similar clients.
