SEO growth / Motorcycle marketplace

Building organic visibility for a new motorcycle marketplace

When I joined this new Serbian marketplace in July 2026, the goal was to reach buyers searching for used motorcycles on Google. The brand was also missing from our initial AI Mode and ChatGPT checks. We worked on listing URLs, technical fixes and content that answered buyers’ questions. I tracked priority searches, interviewed people in the industry and wrote for motorcycle publications to introduce the brand to readers.

How organic Google Search changed during the project

The marketplace relied on individual listing pages. I found that buyers searching for a motorcycle type or brand had no clear, lasting page to land on. We organized the category and brand pages and wrote descriptions for them, giving buyers a place to browse the available motorcycles even after individual listings expired.

What I delivered

Measurement and competitor research

I regularly tracked priority keyword positions and separated branded from non-branded searches. I reviewed competitors’ backlinks and media placements to identify publishers already reaching motorcycle buyers.

Content planning and writing

I developed five title and meta templates and a 24-topic calendar, then wrote blog posts using first-hand stakeholder interviews to give buyers practical, trustworthy information.

Digital PR on a small budget

I selected relevant motorcycle portals and wrote PR and co-branded articles. Working within a modest budget, we secured placements, including a Moto Magazin guide about buying used motorcycles online. I also secured coverage in a local publication with an article on safe riding and how motorcyclists can protect themselves on the road.

Establishing the brand

I added the marketplace to Wikidata, created its X profile and set up Bing Webmaster Tools to monitor search visibility.
