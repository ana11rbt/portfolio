Editorial QA / Technical content

How I used AI agents to produce 80 technical articles

I used Claude Cowork and around ten AI agents to write and edit approximately 80 articles on AI app builders and app development. Agents supported research and drafting in batches. I reviewed the technical instructions, revised the prose, optimized the articles in Surfer and created visuals around the reader’s task.

Inside my editorial workflow

Coordinate research and drafting

I used the team-developed Content Brain for terminology, audience and editorial rules. I organized the work into batches in Claude Cowork, then checked each draft against its individual brief.

Verify technical details

I checked product features, pricing claims and step-by-step instructions against sources. Where a draft gave vague advice, I rewrote it around an example the reader could follow.

Review search coverage and readability

I used Surfer to spot gaps and Hemingway to flag hard-to-read sentences. AI-detection reports prompted a second editorial pass. I used my judgment to revise repetitive phrasing and improve the flow.

Create visuals for each guide

I created diagrams, interface mockups and editorial images for specific steps. When the reader needed to recognize a real product or feature, I used a genuine screenshot.
