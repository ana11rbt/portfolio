AI search / Prompts & citations

Building AI visibility around buyer questions

I help brands understand how buyers discover them in AI answers and use the questions people ask to guide content. For a new motorcycle marketplace, that work started with a small PR budget. I targeted publications buyers already read and wrote a guide to buying a used motorcycle online.

I followed developments in AI search to decide what to test, set up Bing Webmaster Tools, added the brand to Wikidata and created its X profile. I also worked on image formats to help listing pages load faster on mobile.

How prompt tracking shapes my content decisions

Monitor recommendations and sources

Across current client projects I use Semrush and other prompt-tracking tools to follow shortlisting questions, compare the brands mentioned and review the linked sources. I record the platform and session context with each observation.

Choose the next content update

I group prompts by what the buyer needs to decide. A recurring comparison can justify a guide, while questions about how a service works can reveal a gap on an existing page. I revisit comparable prompts after updates.
